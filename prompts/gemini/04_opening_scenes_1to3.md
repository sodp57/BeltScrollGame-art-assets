## 복사할 프롬프트 (장면 1 — 첫 메시지)

```
I need three opening cutscene backgrounds for a 2D pixel art beat 'em up. The attached image shows the real in-game sprites and one in-game background:
match this exact pixel art style, palette approach and character designs in all three images.

Rules for ALL three images:
- 16:9 landscape (1536x864 if possible).
- True pixel art with HARD EDGES, drawn as if on a 384x216 pixel grid and enlarged 4x: every pixel is a crisp 4x4 block,
  flat color clusters, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no soft gradients, no painterly strokes, no photographic texture, no noise.
- Limited palette (about 40 colors per image).
- Keep the bottom quarter of the image dark and simple (subtitles will be placed there).
- NO text, NO letters, NO logo, NO watermark, NO border, NO UI.

Image 1 of 3 — "The fall of Valeria": a medieval kingdom burning at night. In the middle distance a great castle wall crumbles in flames,
towers collapsing, orange fire and black smoke against a dark red sky. In the foreground, small silhouettes of fleeing townspeople
and a fallen banner (blue with a gold winged emblem). Mood: despair, loss.
```
